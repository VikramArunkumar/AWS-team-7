# Communication and Escalation Procedure (SYNTHETIC)

Document ID: POL-COMM-003
Owner: Nursing Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-02-02
Status: Synthetic policy for the CareBridge demo. Not real hospital policy.

## Purpose

Define how staff communicate and escalate when the internal messaging or paging
system is degraded or unavailable during an outage.

## Communication fallback order

1. In-person communication within the unit.
2. Unit-based overhead or runner communication for urgent items.
3. Charge nurse acts as the single coordination point for the unit.
4. Hospital-issued backup phones for inter-unit contact.

## Escalation tiers

- Tier 1 — Routine: note in CareBridge for charge-nurse review.
- Tier 2 — Urgent: notify the charge nurse directly and without delay.
- Tier 3 — Emergency: follow the existing rapid-response or code process using
  the standard emergency line. Outage procedures never replace emergency
  response.

## Rules

1. Every escalation is recorded with the time, who was notified, and the outcome.
2. The charge nurse confirms receipt of every Tier 2 and Tier 3 escalation.
3. AI recommendations may suggest an escalation, but a human must approve it
   before any action is taken.
