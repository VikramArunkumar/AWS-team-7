# Staffing Disruption Procedure (SYNTHETIC)

Document ID: POL-STAFF-005
Owner: Nursing Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-02-10
Status: Synthetic policy for the CareBridge demo. Not real hospital policy.

## Purpose

Define how the charge nurse manages staffing when a disruption (call-out, surge,
or an outage that slows workflow) creates a gap in coverage.

## Assessment

1. The charge nurse reviews current assignments, acuity, and available staff.
2. Identify units or patients at risk from the coverage gap.

## Actions in order

1. Rebalance assignments within the unit based on acuity.
2. Request float or resource-pool staff through the staffing coordinator.
3. Prioritize time-critical care and defer non-urgent tasks with documentation.
4. If a safe staffing ratio cannot be met, escalate to the nursing supervisor.

## Documentation

1. Record staffing gaps, actions taken, and escalations in CareBridge.
2. Note any task deferred for staffing reasons and the plan to complete it.

## Human oversight

- CareBridge may suggest task priorities and reassignments, but the charge nurse
  approves every assignment. The system does not assign staff autonomously.
