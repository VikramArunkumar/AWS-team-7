# EHR Outage Procedure (SYNTHETIC)

Document ID: POL-EHR-001
Owner: Nursing Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-01-15
Status: Synthetic policy for the CareBridge demo. Not real hospital policy.

## Purpose

Define the steps nursing staff follow when the electronic health record (EHR) is
unavailable during a declared outage.

## Activation

1. The charge nurse declares an EHR outage in CareBridge and records the start
   time and affected systems.
2. All units switch to downtime procedures until the charge nurse records that
   the outage is resolved.

## During the outage

1. Continue delivering essential patient care without interruption.
2. Capture all patient updates in CareBridge or on the approved paper downtime
   forms. Do not rely on memory.
3. Record the original time of each observation, not the time of later data
   entry.
4. Flag any safety concern to the charge nurse immediately.

## Data integrity

- Original nurse notes are never altered. Corrections are appended as new
  entries with a timestamp and author.
- AI-generated summaries are advisory and must be reviewed by a human before any
  action is taken.

## Recovery

1. When the EHR is restored, the charge nurse records the end time.
2. Downtime documentation is reconciled into the EHR within the shift.
3. Any unresolved tasks are handed off explicitly at shift change.
