# Manual Documentation Procedure (SYNTHETIC)

Document ID: POL-DOC-004
Owner: Health Information Management
Last reviewed: 2026-01-28
Status: Synthetic policy for the CareBridge demo. Not real hospital policy.

## Purpose

Define how staff document patient care on paper (or in CareBridge) when the EHR
is unavailable, and how that documentation is later reconciled.

## What to document

1. Vital signs and assessments at their actual observation time.
2. Medications given on the paper downtime MAR.
3. Intake and output, procedures, and any patient events.
4. Communications and escalations with time and outcome.

## How to document

1. Use approved downtime forms or CareBridge update entries.
2. Write legibly and sign each entry with name, role, and time.
3. Record the original observation time, not the transcription time.
4. Never erase. Draw a single line through an error, initial it, and continue.

## Integrity rules

- Original entries are preserved verbatim. Later corrections are added as new,
  timestamped entries.
- AI-generated handoff summaries are drafts for human review and are stored
  separately from the original notes.

## Reconciliation

1. Transcribe downtime documentation into the EHR when restored, preserving the
   original times.
2. Retain the paper originals per the records-retention schedule.
