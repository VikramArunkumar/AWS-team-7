# Medication-System Outage Procedure (SYNTHETIC)

Document ID: POL-MED-002
Owner: Pharmacy and Nursing Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-01-20
Status: Synthetic policy for the CareBridge demo. Not real hospital policy.

## Purpose

Define how nursing staff manage medication administration when the electronic
medication administration record (eMAR) or automated dispensing system is
unavailable.

## Scope and limits

- This procedure covers process and documentation only.
- It does not authorize any change to a medication order. Orders are changed only
  by a licensed prescriber through the approved process.
- CareBridge and its AI features never prescribe, never change medication orders,
  and never make clinical decisions.

## During the outage

1. Use the most recent verified medication record available (printed MAR or the
   last known eMAR snapshot) to confirm what is due.
2. Before administering, independently verify the five rights with a second
   qualified nurse when required by unit policy.
3. If the timing of a prior PRN or scheduled dose cannot be confirmed, do not
   re-dose. Escalate to the charge nurse and prescriber to verify before acting.
4. Record each administration on the approved paper downtime MAR with time,
   drug, dose, route, and administering nurse.

## Missed or delayed doses

1. Document any missed or delayed dose and the reason.
2. Escalate time-critical medications (for example anti-infectives and
   anticoagulants) to the charge nurse immediately.

## Recovery

1. Reconcile the paper downtime MAR into the eMAR when restored.
2. Report any discrepancy through the medication-event process.
